BEFORE YOU SCAN

A small, deliberate read.

  • Only starts after you press the button below; reads at most 20 messages labeled Inbox.
  • Scanning and Jev analysis do not change Gmail. Only after you select messages and click Apply selected labels will the extension create missing Inbox Signal labels and add them to those messages.
  • Scanning requests Gmail read-only access. Applying labels separately requests Google's broader gmail.modify permission, which may be described as allowing read, compose, and send access. Inbox Signal uses that extra permission only to create/apply selected labels; it does not compose or send email.
  • Recipient matching happens locally. Jev receives only a relationship label such as “To me” or “not listed in To/Cc,” not the recipient addresses.
  • For analysis, message subject, sender, a text-only excerpt, and link text/hostnames go through the local relay to TypeSafe Jev. Attachments are not analyzed; URL-like text and link paths/query strings are reduced to hostnames before Jev sees them.
  • Email text can contain personal details, one-time codes, or other sensitive information. Scan only messages whose captured text you are comfortable sending to TypeSafe.
  • Messages and results are not saved by the extension. Not listed in To/Cc can also mean Bcc, forwarding, or an unlisted alias—not necessarily a misdelivery.
